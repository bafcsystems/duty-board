Calendar reminders automatic messages before events

Events come from the company Google calendars (one per type). Add and edit events in Google Calendar; changes reach the TVs and these reminders within 15 minutes. Each type below has a default reminder. To change one event, either use the list at the bottom, or add a line to the Google event's description:

  • Notify: none no reminder for this event
  • Notify: Career Staff, 1 day or Notify: BOD, 2 days, morning of who and when

Reminder times: 2d 2 days before at the event's time · 1d@18:00 day before at 1800 · 0d@07:00 morning of at 0700 · 3h 3 hours before. Meeting links (Zoom) in the event description go in the reminder only, never on the TVs. Members can turn reminders off in My alerts; quiet hours hold pushes and texts.

Checklists

New member

  1. Add them in Personnel with the email they will sign in with, certifications, fire quals, platoon and type.
  2. Make sure they are in ESO Scheduling; put their ESO ID in Personnel so shifts match.
  3. Add them to any custom Groups (BOD, committees).
  4. Send them the member how-to (below). They sign in with an emailed code, then set My alerts; on iPhone they must Add to Home Screen first.
  5. Give extra Access only if their job needs it.

Member leaving

  1. In Personnel, open them, untick Active, Save. This signs them out everywhere, removes phone alerts, takes them out of every group and access role, and cancels anything waiting to send to them.
  2. Remove them from ESO Scheduling and the HR file as usual.
  3. If they had a station key, radio or account outside this system, follow the normal return list.

Lost or replaced station TV

  1. Remove it in Screens.
  2. In Personnel, open the screen account and press Sign out all devices; then sign the other TVs back in once.

Member how-to (send this to new members)

  1. Open bafcsystems.github.io/duty-board and enter the email the company has on file. Type the 6-digit code from the email.
  2. iPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and open the board from that icon. (Needed for phone alerts.) Android and computers work in the browser.
  3. Open Messages → My alerts: choose email, text and/or push, turn on Turn on alerts on this device, pick EMS/Fire dispatch alerts, weather, event reminders, the Monday digest and your quiet hours.
  4. When a dispatch alert arrives, tap it, then tap your ETA (5, 10, 15+ minutes or Direct to scene). Everyone can see who is responding.
  5. To report you are unavailable, or add yourself to the board, use the buttons on the On duty tab.
